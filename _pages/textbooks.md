I write free, open-access textbooks 
for the graduate courses I teach. 
They're aimed at closing the gap 
between the math undergraduate 
engineers learn and what graduate-level 
coursework and research actually demand. 
All books are released under a Creative 
Commons license and are free to download.

## Incompressible Fluid Mechanics

A graduate-level textbook on incompressible 
fluid mechanics, developed for a 
first-year graduate fluid mechanics course. 
In continuous use at the Colorado School of Mines
since 2018, and revised annually based on 
student and instructor feedback.

[View repository and download PDF →](https://github.com/nilstilton/FluidMechBook)

## Engineering Math

A graduate-level textbook on mathematical 
methods for engineers, covering ordinary 
and partial differential equations, Fourier transforms, 
and an introduction to numerical methods.

[View repository and download PDF →](https://github.com/nilstilton/EngineeringMathBook)
